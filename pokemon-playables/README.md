<details>
<summary><strong>How to read the format labels</strong></summary>

- **Both:** legal in Expanded and GLC; usefulness still depends on the deck.
- **GLC + type:** included for that GLC type. These 40 Pokémon are also Expanded-legal, but are not necessarily Expanded staples.
- **GLC only:** legal in GLC but banned in Expanded.
- **Expanded only:** excluded from GLC by its rules or ban list.
- **Expanded focus:** included for Expanded utility; read the note for any GLC relevance.

GLC uses one Pokémon type and one card per name except Basic Energy, with Rule Box and ACE SPEC exclusions and its own ban list. Expanded means the physical BW-on format here; Pokémon TCG Live may implement a different card pool. [GLC rules](https://gymleaderchallenge.com/guide)

| Card/version | What to do |
|---|---|

## Sources and selection

Expanded selections draw on Limitless's published lists from **CL Aichi Open League, May 10, 2026**, which had 926 players. The accessible card breakdown covers published archetype lists, not all 926 players; for example, its Regidrago breakdown uses nine lists. Japanese tournament results are useful evidence of card roles, not a direct measure of U.S. demand. [Event](https://limitlesstcg.com/tournaments/566) · [Card breakdown](https://limitlesstcg.com/tournaments/566/cards)

The principal Expanded examples are [Shadow Rider Calyrex, 1st](https://limitlesstcg.com/decks/list/26807), [Absol Garbodor, 8th](https://limitlesstcg.com/decks/list/26814), and [Regidrago, 10th](https://limitlesstcg.com/decks/list/26816). These support the core engines and shared Trainers; this reference does not reproduce those decks or claim to cover every competitive archetype.

GLC selections use the official type guides and example decks, plus CardBoard Warriors' [Hydro Dance](https://cardboardwarriors.net/spotlight/glc-modern-hydro-pump/) and [Munkidori Psychic](https://cardboardwarriors.net/spotlight/glc-munkidori-psychic-brooke/) analyses. Older deck articles must be read alongside the current ban list. Individual card identities and Expanded status were checked against all 110 linked Limitless card pages.

### Maintaining this reference

When updating an entry, check the exact card printing, current format legality, and a deck list or guide supporting its use. Record the research date. Reprints with identical text can be noted alongside the existing entry rather than counted as a new playable.

Corrections should identify the card, set/number, proposed change, and supporting source. Review older deck lists against the current ban list before adding their cards.
