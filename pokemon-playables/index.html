Sources & format notes

Standard: The 2026 rotation took effect April 10, 2026. Regulation mark G rotated; H, I, and J are legal, along with future marks. Older printings of a card can still be legal when an identical current printing is legal. See the official rotation announcement. Standard selections here emphasize cards appearing in top-performing 2026 World Championships archetypes rather than every legal card.

Expanded / GLC: The existing reference remains a curated selection drawn from published Expanded deck lists, the official GLC guides, and community deck analysis. Expanded refers to the physical BW-on format. Check the GLC ban list and linked card pages for current legality and printing details.

Format labels indicate where a card is useful in this reference; they are not a universal power ranking. The “Search my eBay” links search only Dan’s Poke Playables and More for that card, so they remain useful when individual listing IDs change after a sale or relist. Reviewed September 19, 2026.
